## Evaluation Rubric for Language QA Samples

All samples in this repository are evaluated using the following criteria:

### 1. Accuracy
Does the output preserve the meaning of the original text without distortion?

### 2. Fluency
Does the Igbo or English output sound natural to native speakers?

### 3. Tone Appropriateness
Is the tone consistent with the intended context (formal, instructional, conversational)?

### 4. Consistency
Are key terms translated or applied consistently across similar contexts?

### 5. Cultural Relevance
Does the output reflect natural usage patterns in real-world communication?

---

Each sample in this repository references one or more of these criteria in its notes.
