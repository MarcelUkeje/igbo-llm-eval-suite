### Localization Decisions

- “placed successfully” translated as “E tinyela nke ọma” to maintain natural transactional tone in Igbo UI context.
- “Estimated delivery time” adapted to “Oge nnyefe a tụrụ anya ya” for clarity and natural phrasing.
- “Track your order” localized as “Soro ọnọdụ iwu gị” to reflect actionable instruction rather than literal translation.
- Focus was on usability and clarity under time-sensitive user conditions.
